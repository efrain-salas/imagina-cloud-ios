Imagina Cloud brings your company's files to your iPhone and iPad: your team's folders, your documents and your photos, up to date and safe in Imagina's cloud.

With Imagina Cloud you can:
• Open your company's files and the folders you share with your team.
• Upload photos, videos and documents, and scan paper with the camera.
• Turn on automatic upload of your photos, so you never lose one.
• Keep files offline and have them at hand even without signal.
• Share files with your team, or with your customers through a link.
• Set up your company email in the iPhone's Mail app with a link, without typing servers or passwords.

You sign in with your mobile number and a code we send you by SMS: no passwords to remember.

You need an Imagina Cloud account, which your company creates in Imagina. If your company doesn't have Imagina Cloud yet, tell us at imagina.build.

Imagina Cloud is a modified version of the Nextcloud app, made by Imagina. It is not the official Nextcloud app and is not endorsed by Nextcloud GmbH. Its source code is published at github.com/efrain-salas/imagina-cloud-ios.
